Nyx Reminders

A simpler Reminders with nothing taken away. One field to search in the header, and quick add at the top of the list, saying what it understood and which list the reminder goes to. The smart lists become tiles with counts; sync state shows once, at the sidebar's foot. Rows carry their date, repeat, list and #tags as chips, a flag icon for flagged and a thin bar for priority instead of !!!. The details inspector shows every field all the time, with a time dropdown, a List dropdown (so a reminder can move) and Delete as a labelled button. Scheduled is grouped by when. Every shot comes in Nyx Dark and Nyx Light. Click a shot for the full size, or "live" to open the page.
