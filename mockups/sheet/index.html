Inset sheet

Direction C from the Git divider mockups, tried on apps with different layouts. The header, the sidebar and any bar across the foot stay one surface, the frame; the main body is a sheet inside it, with a thin line on all four sides, rounded corners, and 8 px clear of the window's right and bottom edges. Each app is shown today (blended, no lines) and as the sheet, in Nyx Dark and Nyx Light. Click a shot for the full size, or "live" to open the page.
