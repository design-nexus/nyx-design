Nyx Notes

A simpler Notes with nothing taken away. The 55-item menu becomes a command palette ("Go to a note or do anything", Ctrl+K) and a short menu grouped into Note, View and Vault. A note list sits beside the editor and doubles as search results, with highlighted matches and query chips. The editor keeps its reading column; Source, Live and Read are a switch above it; formatting floats over a selection. The status bar goes: words and reading time move to the inspector's Info tab. Graph, Collections and History open as tabs in the window instead of separate windows; Assist proposes edits in the inspector for review; a sync conflict compares both versions side by side; first run is one card. Every shot comes in Nyx Dark and Nyx Light. Click a shot for the full size, or "live" to open the page.
