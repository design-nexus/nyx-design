Nyx Contacts

A simpler Contacts with nothing taken away. The header has search and a labelled New; the "…" menu of ten mixed actions goes. The sidebar sorts contacts, lists, accounts (with each book's sync state and read-only mark) and a Tidy up group for Birthdays and Duplicates. Rows get avatars and a favourite star; selecting several brings up a bar with what you can do to them. The details open with Email, Call, Message, Edit and More; fields copy and open on hover; birthday shows the age and countdown; lists are chips. Edit happens in place, with a date picker and the rarely used name fields folded away. Duplicates compare side by side and imports let you leave people out. Every shot comes in Nyx Dark and Nyx Light. Click a shot for the full size, or "live" to open the page.
