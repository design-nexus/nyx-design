Nyx TV

A Plex-first launcher for Android TV and Google TV, and the desktop Manager that installs it. Three directions for the launcher, each drawn on every screen at 1920 × 1080 (the TV's UI size), and two for the Manager. Rows show one screen in every direction, so the viewer steps A, B, C, then on to the next screen. Dark is Catppuccin Mocha, light is Catppuccin Latte. The media is your own Plex library from Theater's cache; servers, profiles, TVs, firmware, package names and measurements are samples.

A · CinemaThe focused title's backdrop fills the screen, rows sit on its lower gradient, text tabs at top left. Closest to Infuse and Plex HTPC.
B · ShelfA hero with the favorite apps directly under it, then media rows; a centred pill for navigation. Closest to Apple TV.
C · GalleryA calm solid base with an inset hero card, denser poster rows; backdrops only on details. The lightest to draw on TV hardware.
