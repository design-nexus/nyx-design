A cleaner Nyx Git: the header with repository and branch switchers, the five destinations centred and one Sync control; the sidebar as the worktree and branch navigator; one page bar and one inspector pattern on every page. The content is nyx-git's own: its commits, files and the diff behind the last header change. Every shot comes in Nyx Dark and Nyx Light. Click a shot for the full size, or "live" to open the page.
