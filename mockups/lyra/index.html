Nyx Lyra

A YouTube Music app for NyxOS in Nyx Design 2.0, separate from Nyx Music. Four layouts for the main window (A, the reference's layout, is the suggestion), the other screens drawn in A, three expanded players and three mini-players, and Lyra beside Nyx Music in Glimpse, the bar, the dock and notifications. The violet is Lyra's default accent, so other themes and accents restyle it. Every shot comes in Nyx Dark and Nyx Light. Click a shot for the full size, or "live" to open the page itself.
