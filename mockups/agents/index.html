Nyx Agents

A simpler Nyx Agents with nothing taken away. One thing to manage, a session; an agent, a terminal, a worktree and a desktop are things a session has. Each session has one way to stop it and one way out once it ends: Stop while it runs (Force stop if it won't end), then Resume or Discard. A session that never started gets Try again and Discard instead of a dead end. The sidebar has three groups and History; the session bar has the title, its state, where it runs, Stop and a More menu, where the six icon buttons went. The desktop sits beside the terminal, the subagents under it. Every shot comes in Nyx Dark and Nyx Light. Click a shot for the full size, or "live" to open the page.
